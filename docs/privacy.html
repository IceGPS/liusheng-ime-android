留声输入法 隐私政策

最后更新：2026-10-19

核心承诺

留声输入法的语音识别完全在您的设备本地完成。您的录音和转写文本只保存在您的设备上，不会上传到任何服务器，也不会与任何第三方共享。

我们收集的信息

我们不收集任何个人信息。应用内没有账号系统，没有统计 SDK，没有广告 SDK。

权限使用说明

  • 麦克风：仅用于语音输入，把您的语音在本地转成文字。录音文件保存在设备本地。
  • 存储：用于保存语音模型、录音和转写记录到设备本地。
  • 网络：仅在两种您主动触发的情况下使用——①从设置页下载离线语音模型；②您自行配置局域网转写服务器时。日常输入不需要网络。

数据存储与同步

所有数据默认只存在您的设备上。如果您自行配置 Syncthing 等同步工具把数据同步到自己的电脑，那是您自己的操作，数据仍然只在您自己的设备之间流转。

儿童隐私

本应用不针对 13 岁以下儿童收集信息（实际上我们不收集任何人的信息）。

政策变更

隐私政策如有变更，会在本页面更新并标注日期。

Liusheng IME Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Core commitment

Speech recognition in Liusheng IME runs entirely on your device. Your recordings and transcripts are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to any server or shared with any third party.

Information we collect

We collect nothing. There is no account system, no analytics SDK, and no advertising SDK in the app.

Permissions

  • Microphone: used only for voice input — converting your speech to text on-device. Recordings stay on your device.
  • Storage: used to keep the speech model, recordings and transcripts on your device.
  • Network: only used when you explicitly trigger it — ① downloading the offline speech model from Settings; ② connecting to a LAN transcription server you configured yourself. Daily input works offline.

Data storage & sync

All data stays on your device by default. If you configure Syncthing or similar tools to sync data to your own computer, that is your own action, and the data still only moves between your own devices.

Children's privacy

We do not collect information from children under 13 (we do not collect anyone's information at all).

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date.
